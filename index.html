How to move

Drag to move, scroll to zoom, right-drag (or two fingers) to turn. Click a label, or a building that lights up, for its story and photos. C switches between colour and ink, L shows or hides the names of the landmarks; with the names hidden, pointing at a landmark shows its name.

What you are looking at

«Today» is the city as OpenStreetMap maps it. Each year lays an old plan of the city on the ground and shows the buildings that stood then. Which ones stood is read off those plans: an estimate, not recorded dates. The buildings that are gone and the ruins of November 1944 are drawn where the plans show them; their shapes are assumed.
